\textbf{PROJECT NARRATIVE}

This project builds computer models of the heart and circulation for children born with one underdeveloped pumping chamber, who have historically been treated with surgeries that leave them with a single working chamber and lifelong complications. Created from heart imaging and pressure measurements taken during a routine exam, the models apply the physics of the heart to predict which children can safely undergo a complex repair that gives them two working chambers. Replacing today's size-based rules with measures of how the heart actually works could spare more children the complications of a single-chamber circulation and spare others operations that would not help them.
