\textbf{PROJECT NARRATIVE}

The project employs patient-specific biomechanical modeling – Digital Twins (DTs) – to augment the analysis of multi-modality data by including biophysical and physiological information for the patients born with a borderline-size ventricle, who historically undergo a single-ventricle palliation. By replacing heuristic cut-offs (nowadays used) with physiology-based criteria, the approach will contribute to the patient selection for the complex biventricular repair. The patients, after the successful biventricular repair, will be saved from the single-ventricle palliation and all associated long-term complications.
