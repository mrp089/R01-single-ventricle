\textbf{\uppercase{Budget Justification -- Yale University}}\\[.5em]
\textbf{Senior/Key Personnel}\\[0em]
\ulit{Martin Pfaller, PhD, Principal Investigator} (1.0 summer month / 8.3\% effort, Y1--Y5): Dr.\ Pfaller will be responsible for overall coordination and direction of the Yale subaward, mentorship of the graduate student, financial management, and supervision of the \gls*{gr} modeling in Aim 3. He will lead the development and calibration of the constrained mixture model and its validation against the post-recruitment exams. Dr.\ Pfaller has co-supervised ten Ph.D.\ students as a Postdoc and Instructor at Stanford and is the principal investigator on an NHLBI R00 Pathway to Independence Award, and is thus prepared to oversee this project and mentor the graduate student.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Other Personnel}\\[0em]
\ulit{TBN, Graduate Student 1} (12.0 calendar months / 100\% effort, Y1--Y5): This graduate student will focus on model development: implementing and verifying the cardiac constrained mixture model, building the initialization pipeline from the primary site's Digital Twins, and calibrating constituent turnover and gain parameters against the pre-recruitment exams.\\[.3em]
\ulit{TBN, Graduate Student 2} (12.0 calendar months / 100\% effort, Y1--Y5): This graduate student will focus on prediction and validation: running the patient-specific growth simulations, performing the leave-one-out cross-validation and sensitivity analyses, and comparing predicted growth against the post-recruitment exams to assess the model's ability to identify insufficient growth.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Fringe Benefits}\\[0em]
Fringe benefits are calculated per Yale's federally negotiated rate agreement. The G\&C Exempt rate of 28.9\% is applied to the PI's salary. No fringe is applied to the graduate-student stipend.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Equipment}\\[0em]
None requested.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Travel}\\[0em]
Domestic (Pfaller): \$3,000 per year (Y1--Y5). Funds support attendance at national scientific meetings to disseminate findings and remain informed of the latest research in computational cardiac modeling.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Participant/Trainee Support Costs}\\[0em]
None requested.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Materials and Supplies}\\[0em]
None requested.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Other Direct Costs}\\[0em]
Computing Resources (\$3,431 per year, Y1--Y5): high-performance computing resources required for the patient-specific constrained mixture \gls*{gr} simulations in Aim 3, including forward integration of each model over the 8--15 month growth horizon and the repeated simulations required for calibration, sensitivity analysis, and leave-one-out cross-validation. These funds cover cluster compute allocations, high-performance and archival storage for the simulation datasets, and support for compliance with the NIH Data Management and Sharing policy, including curation and deposit of the project's data products.\\[.3em]
Tuition (\$59,913 per year, Y1--Y5): tuition remission for the two graduate students at standard university rates, prorated to student effort.\\[.5em]
\textbf{Indirect Costs}\\[0em]
Indirect costs are calculated on Modified Total Direct Costs at Yale's federally negotiated F\&A rate of 68.5\%, per the DHHS rate agreement dated 02/14/2025. Consistent with that agreement, graduate-student tuition is excluded from the MTDC base. Total costs are \$295,960 per year (\$1,479,800 over the five-year period).
